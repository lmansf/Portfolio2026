Process

  1. Define objective, constraints, and unacceptable outcomes.
  2. Start with narrow agent responsibilities and explicit interfaces.
  3. Add critique and fairness checks before scaling autonomy.
  4. Observe behavior in dashboards and compare against policy bounds.
  5. Tune prompts, schedules, and trigger logic iteratively.
