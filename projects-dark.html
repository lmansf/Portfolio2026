Desktop app

2026

Lighthouse v0.15

A local-first desktop app that answers questions about a small group of files, with citations back to the passages it read. A Rust engine in a Tauri 2 shell handles extraction, on-device OCR and embeddings, and hybrid retrieval. A question over a spreadsheet becomes one read-only SQL query, run by DataFusion and shown verbatim. Use the bundled private model or bring your own key. Installers for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

  • Rust
  • Tauri 2
  • DataFusion
  • Next.js

Forecasting · Machine learning

2026

Attendance Forecasting

Daily theme-park attendance forecast from weather, calendar, and flight-traffic features. A blend of gradient-boosted trees, ridge, and random forest selected by rolling-origin cross-validation beat a tuned XGBoost by about 6% MAE out of fold and cut mean bias from +90 to −7 guests a day. The last 30 operating days are held out as an unseen live forecast with an 80% planning band from pooled out-of-fold ratios.

  • Python
  • XGBoost
  • scikit-learn

USL Attendance Forecasting

An XGBoost demand model on nine seasons of USL Championship matches, on a staged DuckDB warehouse (raw → staging → marts). Two models share every upstream table and differ only by league-position features, so the error gap measures what promotion and relegation will do to attendance from 2028.
