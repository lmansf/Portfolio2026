About

I’m a data analyst in Tampa, FL. By day I keep production point-of-sale systems and their data running at ZooTampa; the rest of the time I model messy operational data into metrics a business can act on, and build the pipelines that move it from source to dashboard.

I came to data engineering through analysis — years of turning numbers into decisions — then earned my Microsoft PL-300 and built end-to-end pipelines and ML projects. I care about data people can trust: clear models, honest metrics, and reporting a non-technical stakeholder can read at a glance.
