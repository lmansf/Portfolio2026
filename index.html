About

I’m a data analyst in Tampa, FL. I came to data through analysis, years of turning esports performance numbers into decisions, and then moved into the systems side: today I own the point-of-sale and webstore platforms at ZooTampa, which puts me on top of the data every ticket, membership, and add-on sale flows through.

My analytics work is self-driven and public. I hold the Microsoft PL-300 and a LearnSQL certificate of competency, and on my own time I build end-to-end projects: an attendance demand model on nine seasons of match data, a fraud-detection pipeline over simulated webstore checkouts, a data-quality-first ingestion pipeline, and a local-first desktop app for asking questions of your own files.

What I care about is data people can trust: clear models, honest metrics, and reporting a non-technical stakeholder can read at a glance, plus the question behind the number: why a channel underperformed, whether a pricing change did what it was supposed to, and what the pipeline missed.
