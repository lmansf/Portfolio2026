About

I’m a data analyst in Tampa, FL, moving into analytics engineering. I came to data through analysis, three years turning esports performance numbers into scouting and tactical decisions, and then moved into the systems side: today I’m responsible for the ticketing and webstore platforms behind ZooTampa’s admissions revenue, from product and pricing setup to vendors and the point-of-sale hardware across the park. That job taught me where operational data comes from and how it breaks.

My analytics work is self-driven and public. I hold the Microsoft PL-300 and a LearnSQL certification of competency, and on my own time I ship data tools: natural-language-to-SQL on DuckDB in the browser, theme-park attendance forecasting, a fraud-detection pipeline over simulated webstore checkouts, and a local-first desktop analytics assistant. I’m looking for analytics engineering and data analyst roles where I own the path from source system to trusted model.

What I care about is data people can trust: clear models, honest metrics, and reporting a non-technical stakeholder can read at a glance, plus the question behind the number: why a channel underperformed, whether a pricing change did what it was supposed to, and what the pipeline missed.
